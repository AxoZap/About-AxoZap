Gaming Page

This page will give a quick recap of my 3 main games that I still often play. 

Geometry Dash

Started around 2 years ago, currently have beat over 200 demons in the game, which is the hardest big tier of levels.

Jumped from Forest Temple, a hard demon, to Stereo Extremeness, a mid extreme demon, over the course of 6 months and 65k attempts.

Hopes to beat Limbo one day, one of the hardest memory levels in the game.

Celeste

Completed all of the main levels, as well as most of the golden deathless strawberries in the vanilla game.

Got into the modded scene by playing Chromatic Complex, a high end expert map.

Have completed a few Grand Master Maps, completed 6eafc, and most of the D Sides pack.

Terraria

Completed infernum mode, one of the hardest map packs in the game, as well as almost completed infernal, a buffed version with more mods. Also played other mods like Fargo Soul and Thorium. 

My hardest achievement was beating Yharon, the 4th to last calamity boss, in infernum, deathless, without rod or mount or hook.
